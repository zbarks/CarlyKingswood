My work

Calm, curious and kind.

Since 2010 I have worked as a therapist with traumatised children and their families, including kinship carers. My practice is built on Dyadic Developmental Psychotherapy (DDP), an approach that treats the relationship between a child and their parent as the place where healing happens.

Much of what I do is help parents and carers read the message underneath a child's behaviour, and respond with an attitude of playfulness, acceptance, curiosity and empathy. This is known as PACE.

I also offer therapeutic life story work, supervision, consultancy and training for therapists and care teams.
