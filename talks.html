Training

Courses and consultancy

I provide training on a wide range of subjects connected with attachment and developmental trauma, adoption, fostering and therapeutic parenting. I also train other therapists in the model I use for therapeutic life story work.

  • Parents and carers
    Understanding trauma related behaviour and building a connected, therapeutic relationship at home.
  • Therapists
    Therapeutic life story work and creative, relationship based practice.
  • Teachers and education teams
    Attachment, developmental trauma, PACE and therapeutic approaches in education.
  • Care teams
    Supervision, consultancy and training for staff, including residential teams.

If you are interested in DDP training for yourself, the official courses are listed on the DDP Network website.
