Rooms and farm

A quiet place to begin

My therapy space is set in peaceful countryside between Bath and Somerset, away from the pressure of school and the clinic.

Therapy at the Farm

Space made for children and families

Therapy at the Farm is a small team of therapists sharing a calm, welcoming site. The surroundings help children settle, and the rooms are set up for creative and relational work, so a child can play, talk or simply be, with their parent beside them.

I offer Dyadic Developmental Psychotherapy and family work here, along with therapeutic life story work. Other therapists on site offer play therapy, talking therapies and coaching.

The Therapy at the Farm website has the full details of the rooms, the team and the other services available.
