About

Who I am

I am a Dyadic Developmental Psychotherapist, consultant, supervisor, trainer and author. I live and work in South West England.

I began my career as a social worker in child protection, including work within the care system. That experience showed me how much a child's early relationships shape everything that follows, and how often the adults around them are left without the support they need.

I went on to train in Play Therapy and later completed an MA in Therapeutic Fostering and Adoption. Since 2010 I have worked as a therapist with traumatised children and their families, and since 2014 I have practised independently from a farm setting in South West England. I completed my DDP training soon after this and am now a DDP Consultant and PACE Trainer.

I was a foster carer for ten years. I also work as a consultant and therapist in residential care and for the fostering agency TACT, so I know the day to day reality of caring for a child who has known loss and fear. I try to bring that understanding into every session.

I am passionate about helping parents and carers connect with their children and ease their emotional pain through the relationship they share.

Approach

Dyadic Developmental Psychotherapy

DDP is a relationship based therapy for children who have experienced trauma and disrupted attachment. The child and the parent or carer take part together, so that the healing happens within the family and continues at home.

At the centre of DDP is an attitude known as PACE: playfulness, acceptance, curiosity and empathy. It gives parents a steady and warm way to respond when a child is struggling, and it supports the child to feel safe enough to share what lies beneath their behaviour.

Alongside DDP I offer therapeutic life story work, parent support and therapeutic education, helping adults understand how trauma and attachment shape a child's world.
